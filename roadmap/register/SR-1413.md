Freeing into a region does nothing and nothing says so at the call: code moved from the heap onto a region compiles and runs unchanged, but `vec.release(list, memory)` and each fresh list in a loop now keep every temporary until the region ends, so refine-build's loop took memory by the square of its step count; its memory check found it, not the compiler or the library. Met writing refine-build (friction entry 13).

Completion: a provider able to say it does not reclaim a single free, and the library's documentation, or a check, telling a reader that `release` into it gives nothing back before the region does.
