Nothing gives out decision numbers outside an item: `item.py start` reserves a block in the clone's item state and `register_next.py` gives rows and questions only, so a change made outside `item.py` takes the next free D by hand and cannot see a running item's block. The gate-redundancy answers took D1647-D1650 from R19.10's reserved D1647-D1666 while it waited on its questions; R19.10 met it merging main and used D1651 onward.

Completion: `register_next.py` gives out decision numbers past every reserved block, and an item's reserved block is checked against the merged tree.
