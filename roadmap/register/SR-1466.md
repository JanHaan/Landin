A `run_logged.py --stop` of an RV64 fixture run under `dev-test.sh` left one `qemu-riscv64` child running, a runtime fixture that had hung for 22 minutes, and the child kept the build lock's descriptors it inherited. Every later `dev-build.sh` then printed `landin: waiting for build lock nix-x86_64-linux-debug` and waited without end, naming no holder: the lock's `.holders` directory was empty, since the child never recorded itself. Met in R16.25; only `fuser` on `compiler/ada/.build-locks/` found it.

Completion: the lock's descriptors are not inherited by the programs a test runs, or `--stop` ends the run's whole process group, and a wait names a holder found by its open descriptor when no record does.
