RV32's frames reserve a home for every fixed scratch register a routine might save, 44 bytes, although D678 prunes the saves a routine's text never names after its frame is laid out, so a frame is up to 44 bytes larger than its saves need; with eight-byte homes and sixteen-byte rounding RV32's frames are 1.1 to 1.35 times Cortex-M0's, and seven corpus programs overrun the reference's 4 KiB stack.

Completion: The frame laid out after the text that decides its saves, or the saves predicted before it.
