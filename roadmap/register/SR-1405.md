A false L0315: inside `loop do`, a list walked by `for below in vec.used(names) do ... end for` and then released with `vec.release(names, memory)` is refused, "the call may change its storage while `below` still views it", although the `for` has ended. D1084 holds a `for` source in use only for a call in the body that can reach another iteration, and D1370 binds `below` afresh on each walk; the check's future-use scan (`Has_Future_Use` in `landin-stages-checking-references.adb`) follows the outer loop's back edge into the `for` and does not stop at its rebinding, as arm bindings stop at `Pattern_Block`. Refused with `loop ... break when`, `while` and a list declared outside; accepted with an unconditional `break` or no outer loop. Met writing refine-build (friction entry 3), worked around with a function per pass.

Completion: the release accepted, with a negative fixture still refusing a release inside the `for`'s body and positive fixtures for the loop forms.
