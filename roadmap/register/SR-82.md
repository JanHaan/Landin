`platform/linux` gives every Linux system call by number with one raw call that takes and returns integers, and typed wrappers for most of the calls the library itself uses; a program calling anything else converts its own pointers, flags and structures, and the checker sees none of them. `hosted/events` makes its own `pidfd_open` through the raw call (`hosted/events/programs.ldn`).

Completion: A typed wrapper for each call, generated with the numbers or written, with its flags and structures and their layouts checked per architecture.

Kept: No consumer outside the library needed an unwrapped Linux call through R16.21 to R16.24, R18.50 to R18.72, R22.61 and R23.10, and no complete binding was proposed.
