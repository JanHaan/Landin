`platform/linux` gives every Linux system call by number with one raw call that takes and returns integers, and typed wrappers for most of the calls the library itself uses; a program calling anything else converts its own pointers, flags and structures, and the checker sees none of them. `hosted/events` makes its own `pidfd_open` through the raw call (`hosted/events/programs.ldn`), and the runtime fixtures `events-signal-stops-server` and `thread-signal-reaches-events` call `getpid` and `kill` the same way.

Completion: A typed wrapper for each call, generated with the numbers or written, with its flags and structures and their layouts checked per architecture.
