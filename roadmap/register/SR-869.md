Short `u64` routines are large on armv6-m: R16.10 measured `core.line.divisor` at size/auto at 1,382 bytes (646 instructions) and `frame_bits` at 508, against 190 and 32 bytes for the same functions in C under gcc `-Os -mcpu=cortex-m0`; 37.6% of the divisor's instructions are stack traffic, 18.1% address forming, and nine calls go to libgcc's 64-bit helpers.

Completion: `divisor` and `frame_bits` within twice gcc `-Os`'s size, about 400 bytes for the divisor, measured.
