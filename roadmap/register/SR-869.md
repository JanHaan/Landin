Short `u64` routines are large on armv6-m: R16.10 measured `core.line.divisor` at size/auto at 1,382 bytes (646 instructions) and `frame_bits` at 508, against 190 and 32 bytes for the same functions in C under gcc `-Os -mcpu=cortex-m0`; 37.6% of the divisor's instructions are stack traffic, 18.1% address forming, and nine calls go to libgcc's 64-bit helpers. After R16.22, whose allocation leaves an eight-byte value in its home (D1567), `divisor` is 940 bytes and `frame_bits` 244; after R16.27's selection (D1767-D1774), which still keeps an eight-byte value in its home and its arithmetic in the r0:r1 and r2:r3 pairs, 924 and 210. The cost left is the pairs: a held pair, and the 64-bit helpers' operands made where they are, is the next step.

Completion: `divisor` and `frame_bits` within twice gcc `-Os`'s size, about 400 bytes for the divisor, measured.

Kept: R16.27 measured it again and took the homes and address forming around it; the pairs are what is left, and the continuous lane pauses after R16.27.
