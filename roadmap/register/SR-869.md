R13.20's `core/line.divisor` is a handful of `u64` operations and costs 1.4 KB on Cortex-M0 at size/auto, beside 0.6 KB of libgcc's 64-bit division and multiplication; with the derived driver's own `line_registers`, 1.3 KB, the driver's image grew from 14.4 KB to 18.2 KB of the 32 KiB profile. Wrapping the provably safe operations saved 72 bytes; dividing instead of multiplying cost 200 more.

Completion: The divisor's and the encoders' code measured against a hand-written equivalent and the difference attributed to lowering choices, with a size target for short `u64` routines on armv6-m.
