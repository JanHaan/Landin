No allocator both grows and can be reset from where it is used. A region frees nothing one payload at a time (`region_free` in `core/region/region.ldn` ignores its block), so a region made over another region reclaims nothing until the outer one is released; an arena resets by its handle but cannot grow; and resetting a region takes its ledger and its parent. `core/README.md` says a single free returns nothing and leaves it to the reader that nesting regions therefore does not work. Met writing refine-build (friction entry 12), where every routine that starts a shorter lifetime is handed the heap as well as its region.

Freeing into a region does nothing and nothing says so at the call: code moved from the heap onto a region compiles and runs unchanged, but `vec.release(list, memory)` and each fresh list in a loop now keep every temporary until the region ends, so refine-build's loop took memory by the square of its step count; its memory check found it, not the compiler or the library. Met writing refine-build (friction entry 13).

Completion: a growing allocator resettable through its handle, or a region over a region that gives its payloads back at its own release, or `core/README.md` saying which allocator a nested lifetime takes; and a provider able to say it does not reclaim a single free, with the library's documentation, or a check, telling a reader that `release` into it gives nothing back before the region does.

Merged: SR-1413 (register sweep of 2026-11-21).
