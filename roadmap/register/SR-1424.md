A float constant is made again in every block that uses it, through a general register, `movabs` and `movq`, since values are block-local and x86-64 has no constant pool: mandelbrot's `in_set` loop rebuilds `2.0` and `4.0` every iteration, four of its 25 instructions, at size and speed. arm64 makes a constant with `fmov` or a literal where it can.

Completion: A float constant read from a read-only pool as a memory operand, or held in an XMM register across the loop, measured on mandelbrot.
