`f(lenof "abcde")` and `f(usize(lenof "abcde"))` are refused with L0103, "a call's arguments are never closed", pointing at the string literal, though every parenthesis is closed; `f(lenof s)` of a slice `s` is accepted. Met while writing a test program in R16.24. What is wrong, and what to write instead, the message does not say.

Completion: `lenof` of a string literal is accepted in a call's arguments, or refused with a message that names it and says what to write.
