An `esp-app-image` is RAM-only (D683): the ROM loads every segment into SRAM, so an image is bounded by the SRAM the description gives code and data, and flash execute-in-place, which needs the ROM's or a second stage's cache and MMU set-up, is not built. Left out of R12.40, as Jan decided.

Completion: Flash-mapped text and rodata, the cache and MMU set up before reset, and the image's flash segments placed where the ROM maps them.

Kept: No ESP program outgrew its SRAM through R16.21 to R16.24, R18.50 to R18.72, R22.61 and R23.10; R16.23 changed Xtensa selection, not where images execute.
