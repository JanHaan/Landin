Choosing the ESP32-C5's UART0 clock for R13.20's line changes, the C5's TRM v1.1 gives PCR_UART0_SCLK_SEL as 0 none, 1 PLL_F80M, 2 RC_FAST, 3 XTAL with XTAL the reset value, while ESP-IDF v6.1's `pcr_reg.h` and the generated module's reset value, 0x0040_0000, give 0 XTAL, 1 RC_FAST, 2 PLL_F80M. Neither the policy nor the correspondence records the disagreement, so a reader of `devices/generated/esp32c5/pcr` cannot tell which encoding the chip has; the port took its clock from the ROM's divisor instead (D961).

Completion: The disagreement decided on the board, by reading the register a known source leaves, and recorded in the policy with the source that settles it.
