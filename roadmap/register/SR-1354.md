`scripts/unit_closure.py --cut PARENT CHILD` reports a child unit's closure without the parent's body, but an Ada partition that holds a child holds its parent's body too, and with it everything that body withs (D1423). So a cut parent makes a child look small that a real link of it would not be: `Landin.Commands.Devices` measured with `--cut Landin.Commands` shows about 22 units, where a program withing it links the 148 units `Landin.Commands`' body brings. Met in the R18 structure check, measuring the split commands.

Completion: a cut that a link cannot honour, a parent of a unit the closure keeps, is refused or reported beside the result, so a measurement never claims a closure no executable could have.
