An atom `match` re-tests its subject's set at every arm, so `failure.words`'s 32 arms cost about 6.3 KiB of Cortex-M0 code at size/auto, about 200 bytes an arm. R13.72 tests the subject once, through a sealed slot the backends do not revalidate, which took `failure.words` on Cortex-M0 at size/auto from 6,336 to 3,552 bytes; the arms remain a compare-and-jump chain of about 110 bytes each, because a table needs a multiway IR terminator.

Completion: A multiway IR terminator and an atom `match` lowered through it as a table or jump, with the image measured.
