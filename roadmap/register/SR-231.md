An atom `match` re-tests its subject's set at every arm. R16.10 measured `failure.words` at Cortex-M0 armv6-m size/auto at 4,336 bytes for 34 arms, about 127 bytes an arm, 69% of its 1,984 instructions stack traffic and address forming, each arm a range compare-and-jump with a far branch; a table of 34 address and length pairs would be 272 bytes. About three quarters of each arm is what register allocation takes, not the chain: after R16.22 (D1567) it is 1,748 bytes, about 51 an arm.

Completion: A multiway IR terminator and an atom `match` lowered through it as a table or jump, with the image measured.
