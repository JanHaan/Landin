The Cortex-M backend allocates only r0 to r7 at every level, so at the Thumb-2 levels, whose 32-bit encodings reach the high registers as they reach the low ones, r8, r10 and r12 still hold no value and every value moves through the low registers and frame homes. Left out of R12.30's Thumb-2 selection.

Completion: High registers allocated at the Thumb-2 levels with `armv6-m` byte for byte unchanged, and the corpus, the frame stepping and the source-debugging lanes still passing at each level.
