A comparison with zero is a `cmp` and a branch at every level: Thumb-2's CBZ and CBNZ go unused. Left out of R12.30's Thumb-2 selection. R16.22 took the rest of the row: a Thumb-2 transfer is one B whose width the assembler chooses (D1564), and a kept comparison's value comes from an IT block (D1568).

Completion: IT, CBZ and CBNZ selected at the Thumb-2 levels where their reach allows, with branch-reach controls at each level and `armv6-m` unchanged.
