Since R16.23 places most of an assembly-free Xtensa routine's words in registers (D1627), its frame still reserves what the words no longer use: an 80-byte bank for the logical words and a home for every scalar value and slot, which only a spilled web reads. The prologue is written before the body is placed, so the frame is laid out first. Over the firmware set at xtensa-esp32 speed-auto the routines' frames sum to 1,072 to 3,792 bytes an image, the deepest one 368 to 800 bytes, each with the whole bank; the homes' extent is 58 to 339 bytes an image.

Completion: An Xtensa frame laid out after placement, holding only the homes its spilled webs and its addressed slots use, with the firmware set's stack figures measured lower.
