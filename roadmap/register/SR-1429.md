RISC-V and Xtensa divide an unsigned value by a constant power of two with `divu`, `remu`, `quou` and `remu`, where Cortex-M now shifts (D1626) and x86-64 and arm64 shift or multiply: an RV32 and ESP32 `% 8` or `/ 16` costs a divide of several cycles a time.

Completion: Each selects the shifts, or a mask, measured on the firmware set.
