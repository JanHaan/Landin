RISC-V divides an unsigned value by a constant power of two with `divu` and `remu`, where Cortex-M now shifts (D1626), Xtensa shifts and masks (D1627) and x86-64 and arm64 shift or multiply: an RV32 `% 8` or `/ 16` costs a divide of several cycles a time.

Completion: RISC-V selects the shifts, or a mask, measured on the firmware set.
