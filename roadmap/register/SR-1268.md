SipHash-1-3 of 16 bytes costs 169 cycles on Linux x86-64 at `--optimize speed`, where C takes about 35: each round's `u64` temporaries go through stack slots, about 90 instructions a round against 14, and each byte load keeps its overflow and range checks. On Cortex-M0 it is 6,670 instructions, and an unsigned `% 8` there calls `__aeabi_uidivmod` where a mask would do. Every map operation hashes its key once. Measured in R13.80.

Completion: The round's state in registers, and a remainder by a power of two as a mask.
