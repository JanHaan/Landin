An indexed increment can keep an extra bounds check, and Darwin still has stack homes beyond the scalar value reuse and integer register promotion already implemented. A bound the loop's end does not name is not proved either: fasta's `select_symbol`, `for at in first..<lenof cumulative - 1`, checks `cumulative[at]` every iteration. Merged: R730-04, atomic and barrier lowering is baseline, not competitive. R16.24 measured it: the lowering of each builtin is one instruction or a fence, but every `core/atomic` cell operation is a call, since its scalar entry is an evidence entry D339 never copies, and a copy's own calls stay calls; `threads_started` and `load_relaxed_u32` were 10.2% of binary-trees' samples on x86-64 before the heap and lock took the builtin directly.

Kept: R16.24 took the heap's flag and recorded the rest; no item has measured a hot cell operation since.

Completion: Single evaluation, traps, addresses, ABI and debugger evidence preserved under measured improvement.
