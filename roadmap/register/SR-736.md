The rig's USB host failed three ways in a day. On the front-panel port the cascade's top hub dropped every 30-50 s with no device or kernel error before it, and autosuspend, turned off everywhere, was not the cause; on the ASMedia xHCI at PCI c2:00.0 a Pico 2 halted in BOOTSEL failing enumeration hard-locked a CPU in `xhci_handle_command_timeout` and the controller died, taking the Arty's USB, which shared it, with the cascade; and with the cascade on the onboard connector's second port, a Pico 2 left in BOOTSEL dropped the whole cascade 75 times in ten minutes until it was unplugged. A switch of one port drops only its own device where the host is sound. Met in the November board round.

Completion: The cascade and the Arty on separate controllers, no BOOTSEL on the bus (SR-680), and a run that meets a controller death stopping with recovery left to the maintainer.
