A refusal spells a non-generic alias of a structural type by its structure: an alias `maybe` of the union of `absent` and `ptr u32` is spelt as that union in L0344, not as the `maybe` the program wrote, because the checker's descriptor keeps no alias. Met re-recording `negative/r630-optional`.

A function value given a routine whose signature differs is L0301 "this function has a different signature", naming neither signature nor the part that differs, so a `from held` against a plain `from`, or one `escaping into` against `escaping`, reads as identical text on the page. Met in R13.92 writing `negative/held-result-refusals`.

Completion: A written alias recovered where the refused node names one, and L0301's note spelling both signatures, or the first part where they differ.

Merged: SR-1279 (register sweep of 2026-11-21).
