`hosted/heap` with no thread started costs 83 instructions more an allocation and free of 24 bytes than a heap whose lock path takes nothing: R16.10 measured 37.1 against 29.1 ns a pair at speed/auto on x86-64 (+27%), 7.6% of binary-trees' run. The flag-clear path is two out-of-line calls a pair to `threads_started`, each calling `load_relaxed_u32`, and the callee-saved registers those calls cost `host_alloc` and `host_free`.

Completion: The flag-clear path within a load and a branch of the old heap, measured.
