At `none` the STM32H753 runs binary-search 4.9% (6,286 to 6,592 cycles) and SipHash 0.9% (1,612,373 to 1,626,451) slower after R16.27's selection, though both execute fewer instructions: its M7 runs from uncached AXI SRAM, where how far a home's store stands from the load or store of the same word after it decides a stall, and at `none` every value moves through its home. R16.27 measured it on the board: the firmware mandelbrot at `none` took 15,674,563 cycles with float homes read from sp straight after the core stores to them and 13,261,199 with two address instructions between (D1771 keeps the latter at `none`), and with frame parts read through r2 again at `none` binary-search took 6,352 and SipHash 1,705,255, so D1769's direct access stays. Every `size` and `speed` configuration on every board is faster.

Completion: These two configurations no slower than their R16.24 figures, or the store-to-load spacing at `none` decided as not worth an instruction.
