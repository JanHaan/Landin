Floating-point values are neither written as text nor parsed: R13.19's formatting covers integers. Writing the shortest text that reads back exactly, and parsing correctly rounded, is real work and costs flash on Cortex-M0. The Benchmark Game's n-body and spectral-norm print nine decimal places and k-nucleotide three, which is fixed-precision writing, a different and smaller algorithm than the shortest round trip.

Completion: Shortest round-trip writing, correctly rounded fixed-precision writing and correctly rounded parsing, with their size on the Cortex-M0 profiles measured.
