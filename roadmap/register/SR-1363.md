RISC-V selection emulates Arm's condition flags: every comparison copies its operands into x20 and x21 and a branch tests them there, a float comparison sets x20, x21 and x23-x25 from five instructions, and every addition or subtraction, checked or not, also computes its carry and overflow into x23 and x24 and leaves its result in x20. After R16.21's allocation (D1503) these are 22% of RV64's instructions over four of the hosted workloads at speed-auto, counted statically (mandelbrot 21.6%, fasta 22.0%, binary-trees 22.1%, fannkuch-redux 22.9%: the lines whose destination is x20, x21 or x23-x25), and they keep five registers from the allocator.

Completion: A comparison branches on its operands where they are, an addition computes its carry or overflow only where a check reads it, and the registers freed are offered to the allocator, measured lower against the R16.21 baselines with unchanged behavior and debugger evidence.
