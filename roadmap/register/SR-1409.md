No call copies a `[]u8` into allocated storage and returns a `[]u8`: `mem.new_bytes` and `docs/from-c.md`'s `duplicate` give a `byte_buffer` whose view is lent from the local binding, so the copy cannot be kept, and the working form, `mem.allocate`, `mem.storage_over`, `mem.admit_copy`, `mem.used` and a slice, is shown only in `examples/derived_hosted/app/config.ldn`. Met writing refine-build (friction entry 8), whose `strings.keep` the whole tool rests on.

Completion: a `core/mem` call that copies bytes into the caller's allocator and returns the `[]u8`, or the five-line form documented where a reader looks for it.

Kept: its activation did not come through R16.25 or R23.20, which touched neither R20.50.
