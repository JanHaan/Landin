activation: The next change to a backend's recorded-writes allocation (D1503, D1567), Cortex-M's or RISC-V's.
effort: S
impact: M
---

When a routine's second emission writes a register its plan holds, D1503 and D1567 keep the first emission, with every value in its home, and nothing says so: the build report's `register_count` is what the plan held before the check, and no option names the routine, the register and the position that failed. R16.27's first VFP allocation fell back silently for every float routine, its unrecorded `vmov` into a held slot, and finding why took a temporary print in `Holds`.

Completion: The build report or a debug option naming each routine that kept its first emission, with the register and position that refused the plan.
