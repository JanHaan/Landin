Atomic exchange, add and compare-exchange are refused on `cortex-m0` at every level: Cortex-M0's admission (D510, D690) holds no level's read-modify-write, so a Thumb-2 core's LDREX and STREX, and Armv8-M's LDAEX and STLEX, go unused, and `core/atomic`'s `exchangeable` has no conformance there. The level-dependent admission itself exists since R12.40 (D690, RV32's `a`); what is missing is the lowering. Met deciding R12.30's selection, which left it out of scope.

Completion: The exclusive loops lowered at `armv7-m`, `armv7e-m` and `armv8-m.main` by each ordering's published mapping, Cortex-M0's admission and `core/atomic`'s condition opened at those levels, and executed fixtures on the AN385 and the AN505.
