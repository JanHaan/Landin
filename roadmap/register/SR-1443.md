A question asked by work that is no roadmap item cannot be filed as it stands: `check.py` refuses an `Asked by` line that does not name a work item of the roadmap, and `register_next.py --question --file` asks for `--asked-by ITEM`. Filing the maintainer's four answers to the gate-redundancy survey on 2026-11-20, which had no identity and was told not to invent one, took borrowing R17.50, the nearest item, with a clause saying who really asked; nothing said that was the way.

Completion: the question form names a way to say who asked without an item, such as a branch or a survey, or the Mechanics say which item such work cites.
