Espressif's ESP32-P4, on the maintainer's rig as chip revision v1.3 on board Rev.D: RV32 with F, whose ESP-IDF builds use `rv32imafc_zicsr_zifencei` below revision v3 and add Zc* and B from it, with the hard-float `ilp32f` ABI. No RV32 level has F, no register class holds its float registers, and Espressif's QEMU has no P4 machine.

Completion: The P4 learnt through R12.11's importer, an F level with the `ilp32f` ABI in the C ABI planner and float operands in [1630], revision-dependent extensions as levels rather than one assumed chip, an `rv32imafc` emulator lane for the core, and a recorded board run.
