Inside an `if` or `elsif` arm, `count: usize = text.encode(scalar, room) else begin fail bad_escape end` closes the `if` at that `else`, as [1030] says, but nothing is reported there: the refusal comes later, as L0110 "`elsif` continues no branch that is open here" on the next arm, L0110 "`else` continues no branch", or L0201 on names the arm declared, and none names the `else begin` or says to write `else (problem)`. Met writing refine-build, a 2,000-line Landin program (friction entry 1); found by bisecting with four programs.

Completion: the refusal pointing at the `else` that closed the `if`, and naming `else (problem)` or parentheses as the way to keep it the call's.

Kept: its activation did not come through R16.25 or R23.20, which touched neither how an `if` arm's `else` after a call is parsed and [1030].
