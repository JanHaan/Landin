R16.24's Cortex-M changes (division by a power of two as shifts, D1626, and public routines no longer exposed) made every timed configuration of the RP2040 and the RP2350's M33 faster, binary-search by 7%, but on the STM32H753's M7 binary-search runs 9.7% more cycles at `size` (2,605 to 2,858) and 3.4% more at `speed`, and merge-sort 2.2% more at `speed`, while fannkuch-redux and merge-sort at `size` run 1% to 3% fewer (boards run 44744774465). The same images run faster on the M33, so the cause is not yet attributed: the M7's dual issue, branch prediction or the loop's placement in ITCM are candidates.

Completion: The cause found and these configurations no slower than R16.22's figures, or the cause recorded with its measurement.
