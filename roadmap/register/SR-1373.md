`refine dap --help | head -5` ends with `refine: refine-dap was ended by SIGPIPE (signal 13)` on standard error, though only the reader left: `refine`'s own closed pipe ends it quietly (D1047), and a shell says nothing of a pipeline member SIGPIPE ended. `refine run PROGRAM | head` says the same of the program, which D730 has `refine` report as a shell would. Met in R18.60 paging the adapter's help.

Completion: A program or a dispatched command ended by SIGPIPE ends `refine` with status 141 and no line, as D1047's quiet end, or D730 says why it is reported.
