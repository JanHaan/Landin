Nothing builds the flake on `aarch64-linux`. R23.40 found the arm64 development shell and `.#refine` broken there, GPRbuild's arm64 Linux archive wanting `libgmp.so.10` that the flake's patching of the prebuilt archives did not supply, and fixed it; the gate's flake job runs `nix build` on x86-64 only and `nix flake check --no-build --all-systems`, which evaluates the other systems without building them, so a dependency one architecture's archive adds goes unseen until someone builds there. The README's `nix profile install` takes the prebuilt `refine-bin` on arm64, which hides it from users as well.

Completion: the gate builds `.#refine` and the development shell on an arm64 Linux runner, or the flake job builds both for `aarch64-linux` through emulation, whichever costs less wall time.
