A runtime fixture that pins a panic site computes it by hand, one plus five times the operation's byte offset plus the family's offset, and must be recomputed whenever a byte before it moves: `refine site` maps a site to its line, and nothing maps a source position to its site. Met in R13.79 rewriting `runtime/compiler-unreachable-dispatches`.

Completion: A way to name a site by its source position, in a fixture or through `refine site`.
