`core` has no math functions: `f32` and `f64` have the language's arithmetic and nothing more. The first `core/math` is written in Landin, so its results are the same on every target, with `sqrt` and `fma` as instructions where IEEE makes them exact and the other functions ported from a permissively licensed implementation, musl's and FreeBSD's msun or CORE-MATH, and tested against MPFR's values; it is known to need tuning for speed and accuracy after that.

Completion: `core/math` with its functions' accuracy stated and pinned, and the tuning left measured rather than assumed.
