Reading a wedged RISC-V core's state needs probe-rs's interactive `debug` console: with its commands given as `-c` and its input not a terminal, it runs them and then panics with "No such device or address" at `cmd/debug.rs:444`, so a script has to give it a pty through `script -qc`; its `info reg` names mepc, mcause and mstatus but not mtval, and a board tool has no way to record a failed run's pc and cause. Met finding R12.40's Hazard3 wedge, an interrupt that corrupted a frame.

Completion: A failed board run reading the halted core's pc, mepc, mcause and mtval into its result, through `probe-rs gdb` and the pinned GDB rather than the console.
