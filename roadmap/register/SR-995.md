A firmware program that wants `time.clock` writes SysTick's registers by their addresses and a clock provider of its own: the library has no SysTick module in `platform/cortex_m` and no firmware clock, so prototype 5's counterpart programs SysTick with `compiler.volatile_store`, as `environments/cortex-m/probes/fibre-capacity.ldn` does with an `assembler.block`, and widens a 32-bit tick to 64 bits in its board. Met deriving prototype 5's firmware counterpart in R14.25. Merged: SR-613, the rest of the System Control Space: the STM32H753's SVD describes none of it, so its board application reaches VTOR, CPACR, CCR and the NVIC's enables through a module of its own, `scs`, at addresses read out of the Armv7-M Architecture Reference Manual, though every M-profile board has them at the same addresses.

Completion: The architecture's System Control Space, SysTick included, as a library module, with a tick-counting `time.clock` provider over it, which the counterpart and the STM32H753 board import, or the reason recorded.

Kept: No firmware consumer has needed time, and neither `platform/cortex_m/cortex_m.ldn` nor `core/time` has changed in a way that meets it since R14.25.
