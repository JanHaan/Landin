An `if` or a loop's test is a comparison value and a branch on it, so RISC-V computes it with `slt` or `sltu` into a register and then branches with `bnez`, often followed by a `j` to the other arm, where one `blt` or `bgeu` on the operands would do: D1687 made the comparison itself free of copies, but only the checks inside an operation branch on their operands. R16.25's RV32 `pick` at `size` spends `slt x16, x9, x18; bnez x16, .L2_2; j .L2_3` on `if a < b`. Xtensa, which branches on two registers as RISC-V does, has the same shape.

Completion: A comparison whose only reader is the block's branch is branched on where its operands are, measured lower on the hosted and firmware sets.
