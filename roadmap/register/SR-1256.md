probe-rs single-steps a RISC-V hart before every resume, even after a halt the debugger asked for, and relies on `dcsr.stepie=0` to keep interrupts out of the step; the ESP32-C3 takes a pending timer interrupt inside the step anyway, which disturbed the firmware the board check read until D1254 parked the C3 first (82 of 1,124 steps entered an interrupt on the old firmware). Met finding the C3 cross-check's failures in R13.79.

Completion: The step taken with interrupts masked on such a core, or not taken after a requested halt.
