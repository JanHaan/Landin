A static Linux program spawns a child with a `clone` that shares nothing, a `fork` that copies the parent's page tables, so a large parent pays for every spawn. A spawn with `CLONE_VM` and a stack of the child's own is expressible since R14.40's naked `clone` for threads (D516), and is not built.

Completion: A spawn that does not copy the parent, measured against `fork`.
