A `{}` placeholder takes no width, padding or radix, so C's `printf("%5u")` is five builder calls (`docs/from-c.md`'s `printf` entry), where Rust writes `{:5}`. Met in R13.73. R13.74's `format_onto` (D527) kept `{}` alone; extending it is a choice of the template syntax `format`, `format_onto` and `io.write_format` share, which is the maintainer's.

Completion: Rust's format specification studied and the parts that suit a template checked when it runs ported, `{:5}`, `{:<5}`, `{:05}`, `{:x}` and a float's precision `{:.9}` among them, or each declined with its reason.
