An RV32 handler runs with mstatus.MIE clear until its `mret` (D680), so no interrupt preempts another at any priority: a long handler delays every other, and the CLIC's levels and Hazard3's meicontext priorities go unused. The NVIC nests by priority on Cortex-M. Left out of R12.40.

Completion: Nesting by the core's priority mechanism, with the saved context and the stack it needs stated.
