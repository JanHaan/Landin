With a float unit every f32 and f64 operation loads its operands from their homes and stores its result through r6, two to five instructions each beside the one VFP instruction, and a comparison branches where an IT block would do; no float stays in a register between operations. Nothing is measured.

Completion: A measured float selection that keeps values in VFP registers across operations, or the cost recorded as acceptable.
