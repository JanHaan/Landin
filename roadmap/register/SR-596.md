With a float unit every f32 and f64 operation loads its operands from their homes and stores its result, and no float stays in a register. R16.10 measured `runtime/firmware-mandelbrot` at armv7e-m on the AN500 description at speed/auto: `in_set` is 804 bytes and 354 instructions against gcc's 104 and 28 at `-O2`, its loop 237 instructions an iteration against gcc's 13. After R16.22, which holds integer values in core registers and leaves every float the unit computes in its home (D1567), `in_set` is 588 bytes and the run 4,117,030 instructions under QEMU, against R16.10's 6,164,429.

Completion: A measured float selection that keeps values in VFP registers across operations, that workload and description its measure, or the cost recorded as acceptable.
