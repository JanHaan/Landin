# Q-79 — May a commit that only records cycles baselines reuse the software pass?

Asked by R17.50 on 2026-11-20, for the gate-redundancy survey after it.

`compiler/tests/measure/baselines/*.cycles.json` is read only by
`environments/boards/timing.py` in a timing event's aggregate. Recording it
cost R16.24 a full software gate of about 526 runner-minutes.

**Options:**

1. Leave them out of the software key, with a reader entry.
2. Option 1, and also let the aggregate judge the previous dispatch's kept
   figures against the new baselines without measuring again.
3. Keep the current behaviour.

**Recommendation:** option 1 now and option 2 later.

**Default until answered:** the cycles baselines stay in the key, and a
commit recording them runs every software lane.

**What waits on the answer:** the gate-redundancy change's second part.

**Answer (2026-11-20, the maintainer):** option 1: the cycles baselines
leave the software key, with a reader entry. Recorded as D1650. Closed.
