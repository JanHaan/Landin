# Q-68 — Do R22's sparse matrices bring direct factorizations?

Asked by R22.19 on 2026-11-20, in the rewrite of R22.

R22.19 gives sparse matrices in compressed row and column form, their
products, and the iterative solvers with simple preconditioners, which is
what discretized fields (R22.75) and large graphs need. Sparse direct
factorization, Cholesky and LU with a fill-reducing ordering as Eigen's
SimplicialLDLT and SparseLU and SuiteSparse's CHOLMOD and UMFPACK give,
solves ill-conditioned and indefinite systems the iterative solvers
cannot, and is about as much work again as R22.19.

**Options:**

1. Storage, products and iterative solvers only; direct factorizations
   named as left out.
2. That, and sparse Cholesky and LU with an approximate minimum degree
   ordering, in R22.19.
3. No sparse matrices in R22; R22.19 and R22.75 are removed.

**Recommendation:** option 1. The iterative solvers cover the common
systems of fields and graphs; a direct solver waits for a consumer they
fail.

**Default until answered:** sparse storage, products and the iterative
solvers with their preconditioners, and direct factorizations named as
left out.

**What waits on the answer:** the scope of R22.19.

**Answer (2026-11-20, the maintainer):** option 1: storage, products and
the iterative solvers with their preconditioners. Sparse direct
factorizations are kept in mind as a later addition, and R22.19 chooses
its sparse format so that they fit. Closed.
