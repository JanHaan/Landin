# Q-78 — Should `measure-time` and the boards' timing run on every boards dispatch?

Asked by R17.50 on 2026-11-20, for the gate-redundancy survey after it.

Every dispatch timed three hosts and every board: about 58 runner-minutes
and 19 extra rig-minutes. Five of nine failed dispatches in 3.4 days
failed only in timing, and each was then dispatched again in full.

**Options:**

1. Time only when the dispatch's compiler-relevant key has not been timed
   on the branch.
2. A separate `scope=timing`, run once per item, with boards dispatches
   untimed.
3. Keep timing on every dispatch, and allow re-running the failed jobs
   after a reading of noise.

**Recommendation:** option 1, with option 3's re-run of failed jobs in any
case.

**Default until answered:** timing on every dispatch, with the failed jobs
re-run after a reading of noise.

**What waits on the answer:** the gate-redundancy change's second part.

**Answer (2026-11-20, the maintainer):** option 1: a dispatch times only a
key its branch has not timed; the re-run of failed jobs stays. Recorded as
D1649. Closed.
