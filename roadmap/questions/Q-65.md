# Q-65 — Does R22's arbitrary precision include floating point?

Asked by R22.13 on 2026-11-20, in the rewrite of R22.

You put arbitrary precision into R22's mathematics. R22.13 is written for
exact numbers: integers and rationals of any size, in a layer over
caller-supplied limbs and a layer that grows through an allocator. That
is what pidigits, R22.20's correctly rounded parsing, number theory and
exact geometry need. Arbitrary-precision floating point, GMP's `mpf` or
MPFR's correctly rounded numbers with their elementary functions, is a
second library of about the same size again, used mostly to compute
reference values and in experimental mathematics.

**Options:**

1. Integers and rationals only; arbitrary-precision floating point is left
   out and named as left out in R22.13.
2. Integers and rationals in R22.13, and a further item after R22.12 for
   correctly rounded arbitrary-precision floats with the elementary and
   special functions over them.

**Recommendation:** option 1. The oracles R22's items need are computed
outside the program, and no consumer in the repository or the roadmap
needs such floats in a Landin program.

**Default until answered:** integers and rationals only, with
arbitrary-precision floating point named in R22.13 as left out.

**What waits on the answer:** nothing now; the answer adds an item or
leaves R22.13 as written.
