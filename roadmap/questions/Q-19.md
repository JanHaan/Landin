# Q-19 — May R18.20 close with `Source_Maps` still linking the front end?

Asked by R18.20 on 2026-11-18.

R18.20's exit evidence asks for "`Source_Maps`' closure without the front
end", which Q-13's answer added on R18.10's reading that `refine-dap`
reaches the front end only through `Driver.Library`. Measuring it showed
that reading was wrong in two ways (D1423):

- `Driver.Library` was a child of `Landin.Driver`, and a program holds the
  body of every parent it holds; `Landin.Driver`'s body withs the whole
  compiler. R18.20 moved it out as `Landin.Library`, so finding the library
  now links 4 units of the compiler, 0.09 MB, where it linked all of it.
- `Landin.Source_Maps` writes a map from a `Landin.Stages.Compilation` and
  its IR. It withs `Stages`, `IR` and `Modules` itself, and withed the
  library only to ask whether a module is the library's. The cut took its
  closure from 7.26 to 6.55 MB of release text; the rest is what writing a
  map needs, and no cut of the library's edges removes it.

So the sentence cannot be met by R18.20. A command that only reads source
maps, as `refine-dap` will, needs a reader that takes no compilation;
R18.60's own exit evidence, "`refine-dap` linked without the front end",
already asks for that.

The exit evidence is the maintainer's wording, so whether the item closes
on what was measured is the maintainer's to say.

**Options:**

1. R18.20 closes with the measurement as it stands: `Source_Maps`' closure
   measured before and after, and the reason it keeps the front end
   recorded in D1423; R18.60 gives `refine-dap` a source-map reader that
   withs no compilation.
2. R18.20 stays open and splits `Source_Maps` itself into a writer and a
   reader of the map now, before R18.60 says what the reader must do.

**Recommendation:** option 1. The reader's shape is decided by what the
adapter reads, which R18.60 designs; a split made now would guess at it.

**Default until answered:** R18.20 closes with `Source_Maps`' closure
measured and explained, and R18.60 owns a reader without the front end.

**What waits on the answer:** nothing; under the default R18.20 is
complete, and option 2 would reopen it.
