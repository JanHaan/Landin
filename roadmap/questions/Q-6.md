# Q-6 — Pay for more macOS runner time to shorten the gate?

Asked by R17.50 on 2026-11-17.

The gate's end is now set by the account's five concurrent macOS jobs.
On run 44076856511 the Linux lanes ended 21.3 minutes in and the Darwin
ones 30.2: twelve macOS jobs of about 115 job-minutes on five runners.
Two items' gates running at once share those five runners and the Linux
ones too: run 44062808053, beside R17.10's gate, took 51 minutes. Every
remaining lever on the macOS floor either changes the evidence or costs
money or changes the account, which is yours to decide.

**Options:**

1. Keep the free hosted runners and the five-job cap; the gate stays at
   about 30 minutes alone, longer beside another item's gate.
2. Add GitHub's larger macOS runners, billed by the minute even for a
   public repository, for the longest Darwin jobs (`darwin-host`, the
   Darwin determinism manifests); what they cost and whether they count
   against the same cap would be measured first.
3. Register a self-hosted Mac as a runner for some Darwin lanes, as the
   rig is for the boards; the gate then depends on that machine being up.

**Recommendation:** option 1. The gate alone is at the floor the cap
allows within a few minutes, and options 2 and 3 buy at most those
minutes and the contention between parallel items, at a price or a
dependency the project has so far declined.

**Default until answered:** option 1. Nothing waits on this answer.
